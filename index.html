Mixdown

Version 15

Layout

Line up to six tracks, record, trim and fade them, shape the sound with EQ, and export the mix.

How to use Mixdown

Quick start

  • Under Tracks, choose an audio file for Track 1. Add more tracks (up to 6) with Add track, or add a tone track.
  • Press Play. Tap the ruler to jump anywhere, or use the buttons to go to the start or end or skip 10 seconds. Turn on Loop to repeat a selection while you adjust it.
  • Adjust each track's volume, pan, and fades, then shape the whole mix in EQ and spectrum.
  • Under Export, press Export WAV, then tap Save. If nothing happens, press and hold Save and choose Download link.

Editing

Tap a track's waveform to make it the active track. In Select mode, drag across it to select a section. Then:

  • Cut removes it and leaves a gap; Copy keeps it. Move the playhead, tap the track you want, and Paste at playhead. That's how you move a section to another track.
  • Duplicate places a copy right after the selection, or right after the selected clip.
  • Delete and close gap removes it and slides everything after it left. Silence mutes it in place. Trim to selection keeps only the selected part of that track.

Split at playhead cuts the clip under the playhead into two clips. In Move mode, tap a clip to select it, then drag it sideways to move it. To put it on another track, pick the track in the selected clip's Track menu under the edit buttons, where you can also type its exact start time. Swiping up or down over the tracks scrolls the page, so you won't grab a track by accident.

Undo steps back through your last 60 edits. Edits never change your original files.

Recording

Open Recording and tap Turn on microphone. Use wired headphones so the music doesn't leak into your take; Bluetooth adds delay. Tap the track to record onto, move the playhead to where you want to start, and press the red record button. After the count-in, sing or play along; press the red button or Stop to finish. Each take becomes its own clip, so you can split, trim, move, and duplicate it.

To stop the microphone, tap Turn off microphone or the red Mic on button in the player bar. It also turns off by itself when you leave the app, unless you're recording. Run Measure latency once per phone or mic (speaker on, no headphones). It sets the Timing fix so takes line up with the music. If a take still sounds early or late, adjust the slider and record again.

EQ

Choose what to EQ in EQ for: the whole mix or any single track, each with its own 8 bands. The EQ is locked so scrolling can't bump it; tap Edit EQ to change it, and Done when finished. All bands that are on work together. Pick a band (1 to 8) to see or edit it, and set its type, frequency, gain, and width (Q). Reset band puts one band back to its starting setting. While editing, you can also drag a band's dot on the display. The amber line shows your EQ curve over the live spectrum while the mix plays. The buttons under the display choose which spectrums to show: the whole mix (shaded) and each track (a line in its color). Your EQ is remembered on this device.

Effects

Each track has an Effects section at the bottom of its card: Noise gate, Compressor, Saturation, Chorus / flanger, Phaser, Tremolo, Echo / delay (can sync to the tempo in Recording), and Reverb (room, hall, or plate). Check an effect to turn it on and show its controls. They run in that order, after the track's EQ. Changes apply when you let go of a slider. Reverb and echo tails are kept at the end of an export instead of being cut off.

Tone tracks

Add tone track creates a generated tone you can layer under your music. Choose Binaural (a slightly different tone in each ear; needs stereo headphones), Monaural (both tones mixed, works on speakers), or Isochronic (one tone pulsing on and off; adjust how soft the pulse edges are). Set the Carrier (the tone you hear) and the Beat (how fast it pulses), or tap a band button. Glide the beat moves smoothly from one beat rate to another over the track. Add a Noise bed (white, pink, or brown) under the tone and set its level, or choose Noise only for a track of just noise. Press Update tone after changes. Tone tracks work like any other track: volume, fades, EQ, split, and trim. Pan is off for binaural so each ear keeps its own tone.

Binaural beats only work in stereo. If a phone speaker or app plays your song in mono, they disappear; monaural and isochronic tones survive.

Mastering

In Mastering, type your Target loudness (0 to -20 LUFS) and Ceiling, choose a Hard (brickwall) or Soft (rounded) limiter, and set the Look-ahead and Release. Press Master now: the app measures your mix, raises or lowers it to the target, limits the peaks, and checks again until it lands on the number. You'll see the final loudness, peak, gain added, and how much limiting it took. Turn on Listen to the master and press Play to hear it.

Under Export, pick a Format: WAV (lossless), M4A (smaller, 256 kbps AAC), or MP4 cover art video (your cover art, or black, with the song, square, 9:16, or 16:9, ready to post). Fill in Song details to save the title, artist, album, lyrics, and cover art inside the file.

Editing tags only

In Export, check Only edit the tags of a song I already have, then Open a song (WAV, MP3, M4A, or MP4). Its details and cover art fill in under Song details. Change them and tap Save tagged copy; it makes a new file with "-tagged" added to the name, without re-encoding the audio. Uncheck the box to go back to exporting your mix.

Music players show cover art in their own frame, usually a square, whatever shape the picture is. For a full-screen look, use the MP4 video export in the shape you want.

Layout

The Layout buttons under the title switch between Auto (side by side on wide screens), 1 column, and 2 columns, whatever your screen size. Your choice is remembered.

Good to know

  • Your audio never leaves your device. Files aren't saved when you close the app, so choose them again next time.
  • Open the app in Chrome or Safari, not inside Instagram or Facebook, or choosing files may not work.
  • The Peak reading after export tells you if the mix clips (goes over 0 dB). Turn on mastering to keep the export from clipping.

Tracks

Recording

Recording needs your microphone. Use wired headphones so the music doesn't leak into the mic.

Sing or play at your loudest part. Aim to peak around -6 dB.

Phones record a little late. This shifts every take earlier so it lines up with the music.

Unplug headphones and turn the volume up for this test. The app plays a few clicks through the speaker, listens with the mic, and sets the timing fix for you. Do it once per phone or mic.

The microphone also turns off by itself when you leave the app or switch to another app, unless you're recording.

EQ and spectrum

EQ is locked so scrolling can't change it. Tap Edit EQ to make changes.

All bands that are on work together. The numbers just choose which band you're looking at or editing. Bypass turns this whole EQ off so you can hear the difference. Gain doesn't apply to high-pass, low-pass, or notch bands.

Mastering

Target loudness is the overall level streaming apps measure; most use about -14 LUFS. The ceiling is the highest any peak can reach. True peak also catches peaks that form between samples when the file is converted for streaming.

Export

Open a WAV, MP3, M4A, or MP4. Its details and cover art fill in below; change them and tap Save tagged copy. The audio isn't touched.
